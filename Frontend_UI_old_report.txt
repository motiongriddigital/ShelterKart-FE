audited the homepage and the report page. The main cause is that the pages are built for expert readers and look like a legal registry, not for someone who wants to know whether a flat is safe to buy.

What I found

1. Text is too small, which hurts the 80-year-old most

On the homepage, 128 of 159 pieces of text render below 14px, and about 73 are at 10px or 9px.
The report page has the same problem. Most labels, chips and table headers are 9–11px.
Around 80 text elements use the light grey slate-400/500, which is hard to read for ageing eyes and in bright light.
I couldn’t get a reliable contrast measurement, because my checker misread Tailwind’s colour format. This comes from reading the code, not a contrast test.

2. The main action isn’t visible when the page opens

The first screen shows a logo, a tagline and three feature cards. The search box is several scrolls down.
The header has seven links plus a button. “36 Markets” and “Sample Reports” don’t say what they do.

3. Jargon with no plain meaning

“Docket” appears 31 times, “dossier” 29, “statutory” 14, “variance” 8, “ledger” 8, plus “encumbrance”, “provenance”, “cross-verification” and “RERA/NOC” without explanation.
A young buyer may know “RERA”. Many older buyers don’t know “docket” or “encumbrance”. Nothing explains these words where they appear.

4. Too many labels, all in capitals

About 29 uppercase micro-labels, a status strip and extra badges (“Registry”, “Audit stamp”) compete with the one thing the page is for.

5. The path isn’t spelled out

There’s no plain “how this works: 1 search, 2 see free preview, 3 unlock the full report” anywhere near the search box.
The report is dense: a summary tile row, stats and many sections. There’s no short “what this means for you” answer at the top.

6. Smaller points

Icon-only controls (the account button, the theme toggle) have tooltips but no visible text, and the interactive items are small.
The demo banner and cookie notice stack on top of the page.
Proposed fixes

Homepage and report, same rules for both:

Text: a 16px minimum for readable text. Body text 18px, labels 14px, with no text below 14px.
Colour: darker grey for secondary text, and no more slate-400 for words people need to read. It would be checked in light and dark mode.
Controls: at least 44px tall buttons and inputs, with the label visible next to the icon.
Wording: plain-language labels everywhere, so “Certified Dossier” becomes “Full report” and “Search Public Docket” becomes “Check a property”. Where a specialist term has to stay, a short plain explanation sits beside it.
Capitals: remove most all-caps micro-labels.

Homepage:

Make “Check a property” the first thing on the screen, with the search box at the top.
Add a three-step “How it works” row right under it.
Cut the header to four links: Check a property, How it works, Pricing and Help.
Move the status strip and the “Registry” badges out of the main reading path.

Report:

Put a “What this means for you” summary at the top, with a clear colour and word status, so colour isn’t the only signal.
Add a one-line plain explanation to each section heading.
Make the question lists and the next steps easier to find.
